# Research

Evaluations of alternatives that were not adopted: kcli, OpenTofu's role, the
two tooling surveys, and the vSphere and Proxmox material in
`docs/research/future-backends.md`. Kept because the rejections still inform
decisions — CLAUDE.md's "Already evaluated and rejected" section points here.

One document here is not a rejection: `vsphere-vcsim-2026-09.md` records what
the govmomi simulator answers of the vSphere backend's call sequence, and its
"What the smoke gate cannot assert" section is the scope of the CI smoke gate.

Nothing here is an instruction. Each document records what was measured on its
date, and the `just` recipes, gates and paths it names may no longer exist.
Proposing one of these again without reading the rejection first is re-deriving
settled work.
